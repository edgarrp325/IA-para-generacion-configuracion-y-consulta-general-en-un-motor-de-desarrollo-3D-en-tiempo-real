# Animation Window Guide

The **Animation Window** in Unity allows you to create and modify **Animation Clips** directly inside Unity. It is designed to act as a powerful and straightforward alternative to external 3D animation programs. In addition to animating movement, the editor also allows you to animate variables of materials and components and augment your Animation Clips with **Animation Events** directly inside Unity.
