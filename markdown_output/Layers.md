# Layers

Layers are a tool that allows you to separate **GameObjects** in your scenes. You can use layers through the **UI** in your scenes. You can use layers through the **UI** and with **scripts** and with **scripts** to edit how GameObjects within your **scene** to edit how GameObjects within your **scene** interact with each other.
